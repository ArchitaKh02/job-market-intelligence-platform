# Tech Market Intelligence

### A Global Job Market & Skills Analytics Platform

An end-to-end data science platform analyzing technology job demand, engineering domains, salary trends, and emerging skills across **India, USA, China, Germany, and Canada**.

## Project Overview

The technology industry is evolving rapidly, with new roles, skills, and opportunities emerging across different regions. Understanding these changes can help students, professionals, and organizations make informed career and workforce decisions.

**Tech Market Intelligence** aims to transform job-market data into meaningful insights through data analytics, statistical analysis, machine learning, and interactive visualizations.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze technology job demand across five countries.
- Compare growth trends across different engineering and technology domains.
- Identify in-demand technical skills and emerging technologies.
- Explore salary distributions and regional compensation differences.
- Understand experience, education, and skill requirements.
- Build predictive models to explore salary trends and job-market patterns.
